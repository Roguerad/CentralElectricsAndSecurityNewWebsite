Burglar Alarms & Security Systems in Derby

Central Electrics & Security are a friendly, family run business that has the experience to design security systems to suit customer individual requirements.

Peace of mind

Fail safe systems, professionally installed

We recognise that customers want a fail safe system that will not give false activations or cause other problems, therefore we only install professional systems that we believe can provide this.

With a professional installation service we aim to install with minimum disruption to homes and offices, hiding cables wherever possible.

Already have an alarm or CCTV system that isn't working as it should? We can look at existing systems too, from replacing batteries to tracing faults.
Smart alarms

Control your security from anywhere

All of our systems have the capability to be controlled remotely, so you're always in touch with your home, whether you're at work, on holiday or just in bed.

  • Set and unset your alarm from your phone
  • Instant alerts if your alarm is triggered
  • Check your system's status at any time
  • View your CCTV cameras remotely

We'll set everything up on your phone and walk you through how it works before we leave.
