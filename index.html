A complete service

Electrical & security work in Derby, done properly

As members of the Derbyshire trusted trader and the NICEIC domestic installer we strive to achieve 100% customer satisfaction.

We provide a complete electrical service, whether you're looking to install garden lighting, full or part rewires of existing properties, new installations, burglar alarms and CCTV systems. We strive to provide competitive prices along with the highest standard of service, with all electrical work completed to IET wiring regulations BS7671.

So if you are looking for electrical services in your area, then Central Electrics & Security Limited are here to help! Whether you would like a free quotation or just need a reliable electrician to carry out an installation, to upgrade your fuse board, or to find a fault, please contact us for more details.
